Common questions

I cannot sign in

Please use the email address our office holds for you. If you sign in with an email code, check your junk folder for the message. If you use Sign in with Apple and chose to hide your email address, please let us know so that we can link your account. If the problem continues, write to us and we will help.

How do I cancel or move a lesson?

Open the lesson in the app and choose to cancel or request a new time. The cancellation policy, and any fee, are shown before you confirm.

How do I pay an invoice?

Open the invoice in the app and pay securely by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. A receipt is sent to you by email. If an amount looks incorrect, please write to us before paying.

How do I change my reminders?

Under Account in the app, choose how you would like to be reminded: by email, by notification or by WhatsApp.

Your data and your account

Download your data

  1. Open the Elite Education app and sign in.
  2. Go to Account → Your data and privacy.
  3. Choose Download my data. You will receive a file containing the information we hold about you and, for parents, your family, together with a readable summary.

Delete your account

  1. Go to Account → Your data and privacy → Delete my account.
  2. Read what will be removed. You will be offered the chance to download your data first.
  3. Type the confirmation shown and confirm. Your account is closed straight away.

Your personal details, lesson notes, homework and messages are removed. Invoices and payment records are kept in anonymised form for the period UAE tax law requires. If you would prefer us to close your account for you, please write to hello@eliteeducation.me from the email address on the account.
