What we teach

Every subject, phase and curriculum

Whatever your child is studying, and wherever they are in their education, we provide specialist tuition that is matched to their course and their examinations.

Curricula

The International Baccalaureate, IGCSE, GCSE and A-Level, together with other British, international and national curricula followed in schools across the UAE.

Phases

Primary, secondary and sixth form, through to university entrance. We support students at every stage, from building early foundations to preparing for final examinations.

Subjects

The sciences, English, Arabic, the humanities, modern languages and mathematics, together with early reading, entrance examinations and university admissions. Each student is taught by a specialist in their subject.

Examinations

Structured revision plans, past paper practice and guidance on internal assessments and coursework, so that students arrive at each examination prepared and confident.
